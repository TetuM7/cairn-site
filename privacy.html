Privacy policy

Draft for pre-release use. This policy covers Cairn while it's in development and used only by test accounts. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, and it'll be replaced before Cairn opens to the public. Last updated 16 November 2026.

Who we are

Cairn is operated by {{OPERATOR_NAME}}. For anything about your data, email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

What we never collect

  • Your application's secret values, such as API keys, passwords and tokens for your app. They're resolved and written on your own device, and Cairn's servers refuse requests that contain them.
  • Your app's own users' data, such as the rows in your database.
  • Your source code. When Cairn scans a project, the scan runs on your device and sends only service names, signal types, variable names and file paths.

How we use it

Only to run Cairn for you. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it, except with the services that run Cairn (below) or when the law requires it.

Where it's kept

On Google Cloud, in the United States. Provider tokens are encrypted with keys held in Google Cloud's key management service. Sign-in uses GitHub. The providers you connect receive requests from Cairn under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

While your account exists. Removing a provider's access, for example by removing Cairn's integration in Vercel, makes its token stop working. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete its records, including the tokens.

Your choices

  • Disconnect any provider at any time, from that provider.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or its deletion, by emailing {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}.

Changes

We'll update this policy as Cairn develops. The date at the top shows the latest version.
