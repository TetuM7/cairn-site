Cairn for Vercel

Connecting Vercel lets Cairn show where your app runs, its deployments and its domains on your project's map, and check them.

Installing it

  1. Start from Cairn: run cairn up, or choose Connect on the map. Cairn opens Vercel's install page in your browser.
  2. Choose the Vercel account or team, and the projects Cairn may see.
  3. Vercel returns you to Cairn, and the connection shows as Connected. It shows as Working only after a check passes.

What it can read

Every permission is read-only.

Vercel permissionAccessWhy Cairn needs it
ProjectsReadShow which project the app runs on, and its settings
DeploymentsReadShow deploy status and previews, and what triggers a deploy
DomainsReadShow where each domain points
TeamReadShow which team a project belongs to
UserReadShow which login the account is under
Integration configurationReadSee which projects you've shared with Cairn

What it never does

  • It never reads your environment variables' values. Cairn asks for no environment-variable permission. Your keys stay on your machine, and Cairn records only where each one lives.
  • It never touches billing. It asks for no billing permission, and never changes a plan or subscription.
  • It never buys or transfers domains, deploys, or deletes anything. It has no write access.

Removing it

In Vercel, open your account or team's Integrations page, choose Cairn, and remove it. Vercel revokes Cairn's access at once, so the token Cairn held stops working, and the map shows the connection as disconnected.
